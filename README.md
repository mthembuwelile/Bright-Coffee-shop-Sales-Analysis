                                                      BRIGHT COFFEE SHOP SALES ANALYSIS
OVERVIEW

This project analyses historical transactional data from Bright Coffee Shop to generate actionable business insights for a newly appointed CEO. The main goal is to help drive growth in revenue and improve product performance by identifying sales trends, peak performance times, and top/low-performing products.

AIM

Analyse sales patterns, customer behaviour, and product performance using SQL and data visualization tools, and deliver clear, actionable recommendations for increasing sales and optimizing operations.
 
OBJECTIVES

•	Identify products generating the most revenue.

•	Analyse peak sales periods across different time intervals.

•	Visualize sales trends by product category and time.

•	Provide strategic recommendations for business growth.

SUMMARY FINDINGS 


Sales by Time of Day
*Morning sales dominate (55.56%), more than double the afternoon and 6x evening sales.  
- Lower Manhattan leads in morning sales, while Astoria peaks in the afternoon.

Revenue by Weekday
*Monday (14.55%) is the strongest day, closely followed by Friday and Thursday.  
- Saturday is the lowest, contributing just 13.9% of weekly revenue.
  
Revenue by Store Location
- Hell’s Kitchen leads at 33.84%, but revenue is evenly distributed across all three stores (only 1.7% variation).  
- June marks the peak month; February is the lowest.
- 
Spending Bands
*Low spenders (0–100) drive 90.9% of total revenue.  
- Medium and High spend customers contribute little, showing major growth potential** in premium segments.
-  
Sales Trends (Jan–Jun)
- Revenue doubled from R81.7K in January → R166.5K in June.  
- March–May saw the fastest growth, consistent across all stores.  

Revenue by Product Category
*Coffee leads (38.63%), followed by Tea (28.11%), Bakery, and Drinking Chocolate.  
- Packaged Chocolate is negligible (0.63%).
- 
Recommendations
1. Maximize Morning Peak Sales → “Morning Combo Deals” (7–10 AM).  
2. Innovate Product Offerings → Seasonal pastries and regional flavors.  
3. Boost Weekday Performance → Loyalty “Store Cards” for Thursday & Friday.  
4. Grow Premium Segments → Bundled offers (R250+ with freebies/merchandise).
